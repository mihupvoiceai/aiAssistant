Electric in town. Hybrid on the highway.

A 1.5 turbo-petrol and two electric motors share the work through a 3-speed hybrid gearbox. Charge it at home and most weekday driving costs a fraction of diesel. Skip the plug and it still runs as a hybrid.

Electric

Office and back, silently

An 18.4 kWh battery covers about 90 km on electricity: a full week of commuting for most people.

Hybrid

Highway, with power to spare

Engine and motors combine for 360 hp. Overtakes are short, and the 70-litre tank takes total range past 1,100 km.

Charging

Top up while you sleep

About 3 hours from 30 to 100% on a 6.6 kW home charger, or 30 to 80% in about 27 minutes on a 50 kW DC fast charger.
